## Product Purpose

Bean Boutique is an NCC Education front-end development assignment prototype. It demonstrates how a coffee boutique could present its coffee collection, brewing equipment, workshops, special offers and subscription options through a six-page website.

Success for this deliverable means that visitors can find the required information, navigate the six pages, use the demonstrated front-end interactions, and understand which functions are illustrative rather than operational.

## Operating Context

Visitors use the website to:
- discover coffee blends and single-origin selections, including tasting notes and brewing recommendations;
- explore brewing equipment and usage guidance;
- add sample products to a browser-local demonstration bag;
- review sample workshops and offers;
- interact with embedded third-party content and demonstration widgets.

The repository is a static website hosted on Vercel. The shop's real address, event venue, contact recipient and operating details have not been confirmed.
