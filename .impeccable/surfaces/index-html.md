## Scope and mode
Whole six-page site (index, coffee, equipment, cart, events, subscriptions). Mode: Persuade for home, events and offers; Operate for the catalogue, equipment and cart. Visitor wants to find a coffee or tool, understand it, and act.

## Audience, job, action, constraints
Coffee explorers in Malawi and the region. Job: pick a coffee or tool and start an order or booking. Constraints: plain HTML5, external CSS and JavaScript, no framework or build step; six pages; the brief's required features; sample prices and events stay labelled illustrative; no invented claims.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Every coffee is a graded record. The shop shows flavour the way a roaster grades a cupping sheet, so visitors read measured evidence and order from the sheet itself. It refuses the category default: cream ground, serif headline, bean illustration.
OWN-WORLD: Cobalt cupping-table field (#1f3fbf), lab-white paper (#f4f5f2), graphite ink (#15181c), marigold marks (#f2b233), roast-brown product grounds (#3b2519). Archivo for display, Martian Mono for numbers and labels. One-pixel ruled tables, bar scales, no decorative gradients.
STORY: The visitor understands each coffee has a measured profile, believes the shop grades carefully, and adds a coffee to the bag from its sheet.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed cobalt hero. Left: heavy display headline "Good coffee. Good company.", one-line description, marigold primary action "Find your coffee", secondary text link. Right: a white score sheet for Mzuzu Highlands with six attribute bars, marked illustrative. Primary action sits at the lower left of the hero.
FORM: Degraded roll (no network), seed:7ec97faf330fbd52; assigned candidate 3 of 7 grounded directions. No challengers. Grounded set: chitenje textile system, shopfront signwriting, cupping score sheet (assigned), drying-bed earth, minibus hand-painting, produce-stall signage, philatelic stamp notation.
