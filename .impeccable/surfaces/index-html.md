## Scope and mode
Whole six-page site (index, coffee, equipment, cart, events, subscriptions). Mode: Persuade for home, events and offers; Operate for the catalogue, equipment and cart. Visitor wants to find a coffee or tool, understand it, and act.

## Audience, job, action, constraints
Coffee explorers in Malawi and the region. Job: pick a coffee or tool and start an order or booking. Constraints: plain HTML5, external CSS and JavaScript, no framework or build step; six pages; the brief's required features; sample prices and events stay labelled illustrative; no invented claims.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Coffee presented as a specialty roastery's own editorial world. The shop shows flavour as a measured profile on a cupping score sheet, and it refuses the category default of cream ground and serif headline.
OWN-WORLD: Espresso #1A120E header and footer, coffee #3A2418 secondary dark, caramel #6B4A2E actions, taupe #A88462 detail, champagne #D5C4AE editorial sections, charcoal #212121 text, white #FFFFFF surfaces. Archivo for display and body, Martian Mono for data only.
STORY: The visitor understands each coffee has a measured profile, believes the roastery grades carefully, and adds a coffee to the bag from its profile.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed espresso hero. Left: heavy display headline "Good coffee. Good company.", one-line description, caramel primary action "Find your coffee" with a secondary text link. Right: a white score sheet for Mzuzu Highlands with six attribute bars, marked illustrative.
FORM: Pinned direction. The palette and art direction were supplied whole in the premium website directive, so no concept roll was run. Seed: none (pinned).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
