A few useful details

Good to know.

Can I change my coffee each month?

That would be managed through a customer account in a full e-commerce build. This prototype only demonstrates the plan selection.

Can I pause a subscription?

Yes, this is a proposed benefit. A production subscription system would need to expose pause, skip and cancellation controls with clear billing dates.

When will I be charged?

No charge is made by this prototype. A live service should show the billing cadence and renewal date before checkout.
